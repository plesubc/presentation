## About Abacus
### https://abacus.library.ubc.ca

- Open and licensed data repository 
- Hosted by UBC 
- Partnership with SFU, UNBC, UVic 
- Includes StatCan and DLI data <!-- .element: class="fragment" style="font-weight: bold; color:#800020"--> 


Note: Many of you are already familiar with Abacus. It's a repository for both open and licensed data collected for use by researchers - not an RDM repository for data collected or generated by researchers. It's hosted by UBC Library on UBC servers, but it's a partnership with Simon Fraser University, the University of Northern British Columbia, and the University of Victoria, whose researchers all have access to the licensed data.

Of particular interest in the Odesi context is that Abacus has many Statistics Canada and DLI datasets. We'll take a closer look at that in a moment.

---

## Migration/merging goals

- Preserve access for Abacus users
- Minimuze disruption to Borealis/Odesi 

**Challenges:** avoid duplication, maintain user experience in Odesi 

Note: To put it simply, our goals with this migration are twofold: we want to preserve access for Abacus users with the least amount of disruption to the Borealis and Odesi community. To do this we can't simply migrate everything, we need to merge and deduplicate to avoid clutter and maintain the Odesi user experience.

## Migration strategy
### Three groups of Abacus datasets

data that doesn't belong in Odesi/GeoPortal<br/>   <!-- .element class="fragment" -->
_migrate to UBC Borealis_  <!-- .element class="fragment" -->
<br/><br/>

geospatial data that belongs in GeoPortal<br/>   <!-- .element class="fragment" -->
_merge after 2027 GeoPortal upgrade_   <!-- .element class="fragment" -->
<br/><br/>

data that belongs in Odesi<br/>   <!-- .element class="fragment" --> 
_merge with Odesi collections_  <!-- .element class="fragment" style="font-weight: bold; color:#800020"-->


Note: Our migration strategy identifies three groups of Abacus data. The first group is data that doesn't belong in Odesi or Scholars Geoportal - for example, because it licensed only for UBC users or is otherwise not is scope for Odesi. The action for these datasets is to migrate them to UBC's own Borealis collection, where they will be available to users but won't conflict with current Odesi discovery paths.

The second group is geospation data that _does_ belong in Scholars GeoPortal - for example, DMTI datasets. The action in this case will be to merge the Abacus collection with Scholars GeoPortal, but this will only happen after the 2027 GeoPortal upgrade.

The third group is the one most relevant today: it's data that belongs in Odesi and in many cases is already held in Odesi. The vast majority of these are Statistics Canada and DLI datasets, and our action will be to merge these into existing Odesi collections on Borealis.

Abacus dataset _not_ held by Odesi <br/>
_Migrate to Odesi_ <!-- .element: class="fragment" -->

Note: This one is straightforward: if Abacus has a dataset that's in scope, but that Odesi doesn't yet have, we propose that it be migrated to the appropriate Odesi collection.
